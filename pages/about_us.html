ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Your Health, Our Priority

KAYGEE EMS is committed to providing dependable emergency medical assistance, patient transportation and professional healthcare support to individuals, families, businesses and communities.

We understand that medical emergencies and healthcare needs require more than transportation. They require compassion, professionalism, responsibility and a commitment to patient safety.

Through our medical services, we aim to support patients throughout their healthcare journey, from emergency situations and workplace injuries to scheduled hospital transfers and private patient transportation.
