OUR DEPARTMENTS

Excellence Across Every Emergency Medical Service

KAYGEE EMS operates through specialised departments dedicated to delivering professional emergency medical services, quality patient care, and efficient healthcare operations. Through teamwork, medical expertise, compassion, and commitment, we strive to provide reliable emergency medical solutions when they are needed most.

Emergency Medical Services

Our Emergency Medical Services Department focuses on providing timely emergency medical assistance, pre-hospital care, and patient transportation. Our medical personnel are committed to responding professionally to emergency situations while prioritising patient safety, dignity, and wellbeing.

  • ✔ Emergency Ambulance Response
  • ✔ Pre-Hospital Emergency Care
  • ✔ Advanced Life Support Services (where accredited)
  • ✔ Basic Life Support Services
  • ✔ Patient Assessment & Stabilisation
  • ✔ Emergency Patient Transportation

Patient Transport Services

Our Patient Transport Department provides safe, reliable, and professional medical transportation for patients requiring transfers between healthcare facilities, medical appointments, and other approved destinations. We prioritise patient comfort, safety, and appropriate medical support throughout every journey.

  • ✔ Inter-Facility Patient Transfers
  • ✔ Non-Emergency Patient Transportation
  • ✔ Hospital Discharge Transportation
  • ✔ Medical Appointment Transfers
  • ✔ Stretcher & Wheelchair Transportation
  • ✔ Scheduled Medical Transport Services

Medical Operations & Management

Our Medical Operations and Management Department oversees the coordination, planning, and delivery of our healthcare services. Through effective communication, operational monitoring, personnel management, and quality assurance, we work to maintain professional service standards and efficient emergency medical operations.

  • ✔ Ambulance Fleet Management
  • ✔ Medical Personnel Coordination
  • ✔ Emergency Response Planning
  • ✔ Medical Equipment Management
  • ✔ Quality Assurance & Compliance
  • ✔ Operational Monitoring & Reporting

INTEGRATED MEDICAL OPERATIONS

Working Together To Save Lives

The strength of KAYGEE EMS lies in the collaboration between our Emergency Medical Services, Patient Transport, and Medical Operations departments. Each department plays an important role in ensuring professional healthcare delivery, patient safety, effective coordination, and compassionate medical assistance from the first point of contact through to the completion of care.

Emergency Response

Our emergency medical teams respond to emergency calls, provide appropriate pre-hospital medical assistance, and prioritise patient safety and timely access to healthcare.

Patient Care & Transport

We facilitate safe and professional patient transportation while maintaining dignity, comfort, and appropriate medical support throughout the journey.

Medical Operations

Our management teams coordinate personnel, ambulance resources, medical equipment, and operational activities to support reliable and efficient service delivery.
